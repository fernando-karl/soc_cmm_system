Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the SOC CMM Assessment System

General Questions

What is the SOC CMM Assessment System?

The SOC CMM Assessment System is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to help organizations evaluate and improve their Security Operations Center (SOC) maturity levels. It provides assessment tools, questionnaires, and reporting capabilities based on the SOC Capability Maturity Model (CMM) framework.

Who should use this system?

This system is designed for:

  • Security professionals and SOC managers
  • IT security consultants and auditors
  • Organizations looking to assess their security operations maturity
  • Companies planning to establish or improve their SOC capabilities
  • Security teams seeking to benchmark their current state

How long does an assessment take to complete?

The time to complete an assessment varies depending on the organization's size and complexity:

  • Small organizations: 2-4 hours
  • Medium organizations: 4-8 hours
  • Large organizations: 8-16 hours

You can save your progress and return to complete the assessment at any time.

Account & Registration

How do I create an account?

To create an account:

  1. Click the "Register" button in the top navigation
  2. Fill in your username, email, and password
  3. Provide your full name (optional)
  4. Click "Create Account"
  5. You'll be automatically logged in and can start using the system

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

Currently, password reset functionality is not available. Please contact your system administrator or support team to reset your password. In the future, we plan to implement automated password reset functionality.

Can I share my account with colleagues?

No, account sharing is not permitted for security reasons. Each user should have their own account. This ensures proper audit trails and maintains the integrity of assessment data. If multiple people need access, each should create their own account.

Assessment Process

How do I start a new assessment?

To start a new assessment:

  1. Navigate to the "Customers" page
  2. Create a new customer or select an existing one
  3. Click "New Assessment" for that customer
  4. The system will create a new assessment and redirect you to the questionnaire
  5. Begin answering questions in the Business domain

Can I save my progress and continue later?

Yes! Your progress is automatically saved as you complete aspects within each domain. You can:

  • Close your browser and return later
  • Navigate between domains and aspects
  • Review and modify previous answers
  • Resume from where you left off

Your assessment will remain available until you complete it or delete it.

What if I need to change an answer?

You can modify answers at any time before completing the assessment:

  • Use the "Back to Aspects" button to return to previous domains
  • Navigate to the specific aspect you want to modify
  • Change your answers and save
  • Your updated responses will be reflected in the final results

How do I know which maturity level to choose?

Each question includes guidance to help you determine the appropriate maturity level:

  • Level 1 (Initial): Ad hoc processes, inconsistent execution
  • Level 2 (Developing): Basic processes defined, some consistency
  • Level 3 (Defined): Standardized processes, consistent execution
  • Level 4 (Managed): Measured processes, quantitative management
  • Level 5 (Optimized): Continuous improvement, innovation focus

Be honest in your assessment - this helps identify real improvement opportunities.

Results & Reports

How do I interpret my assessment results?

Your results include:

  • Radar Chart: Visual representation of maturity across all domains
  • Domain Scores: Detailed breakdown of each domain's maturity level
  • Aspect Details: Specific areas within each domain
  • Recommendations: Suggested improvements based on your scores

Focus on domains with lower scores for immediate improvement opportunities.

Can I export my results?

Yes, you can export your assessment results:

  • Use the "Export" button on the results page
  • Results are available in standard formats
  • You can share reports with stakeholders
  • Export includes charts, scores, and recommendations

Can I compare multiple assessments?

Yes! You can:

  • Complete multiple assessments for the same customer
  • Track progress over time
  • Compare results between different assessment periods
  • Measure improvement in specific domains
  • Generate trend reports

This is especially useful for measuring the impact of improvement initiatives.

Technical Support

What browsers are supported?

The system supports all modern browsers:

  • Chrome (recommended)
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge

For the best experience, use the latest version of your browser and ensure JavaScript is enabled.

Is the system mobile-friendly?

Yes! The system is fully responsive and optimized for:

  • Desktop computers
  • Tablets
  • Mobile phones
  • All screen sizes

You can complete assessments on any device with an internet connection.

What should I do if I encounter an error?

If you encounter an error:

  1. Try refreshing the page
  2. Clear your browser cache and cookies
  3. Check your internet connection
  4. Try using a different browser
  5. Contact support if the issue persists

Most issues can be resolved by refreshing the page or clearing browser data.

Security & Privacy

Is my data secure?

Yes, we take data security seriously:

  • All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication and authorization
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access controls and audit logging
  • Compliance with data protection regulations

See our Privacy Policy for more details.

Who can access my assessment data?

Your assessment data is private and secure:

  • Only you and authorized users in your organization can access your data
  • System administrators have limited access for support purposes
  • Data is not shared with third parties without your consent
  • You control who can view your assessment results
