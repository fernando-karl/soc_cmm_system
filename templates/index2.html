About the SOC CMM

The SOC CMM is a framework designed to help organizations evaluate and improve their security operations capabilities.

Maturity Levels

  • Level 1 - Initial: Ad hoc, reactive approach with minimal documentation
  • Level 2 - In Development: Some processes defined, but inconsistently applied
  • Level 3 - Defined: Processes documented and consistently followed
  • Level 4 - Managed: Processes are measured and controlled
  • Level 5 - Optimized: Continuous improvements and optimization

Assessment Process

The assessment covers six domains with various aspects, providing a comprehensive view of your SOC's maturity across critical areas.
