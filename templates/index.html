About SOC CMM

The Security Operations Center Capability Maturity Model (SOC CMM) is a framework designed to help organizations assess and improve their security operations capabilities.

Maturity Levels

  • Level 1 - Initial: Ad hoc, reactive approach with minimal documentation
  • Level 2 - Developing: Some processes defined but inconsistently applied
  • Level 3 - Defined: Documented processes that are consistently followed
  • Level 4 - Managed: Processes are measured and controlled
  • Level 5 - Optimized: Continuous improvement and optimization

Assessment Process

The assessment covers six domains with multiple aspects each, providing a comprehensive view of your SOC's maturity across all critical areas.
