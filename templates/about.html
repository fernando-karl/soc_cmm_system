About Us

Learn about the team and vision behind the SOC CMM Assessment System

Who We Are

Built and maintained by Service IT Security

The SOC CMM Assessment System is developed and maintained by Service IT Security, a team dedicated to strengthening cybersecurity operations. We help organizations evaluate and improve the maturity of their Security Operations Centers (SOC) by combining practical experience with proven methodologies.

Our Mission

Clarity, measurability, and continuous improvement

Our mission is to make SOC maturity measurable and actionable. We provide clear guidance, consistent measurement, and meaningful insights so teams can prioritize investments and track progress across people, process, technology, services, and outcomes.

Why SOC CMM

A structured, repeatable approach to SOC capability development

The Security Operations Center Capability Maturity Model (SOC CMM) offers a structured framework to assess current capabilities, identify gaps, and plan improvements. This platform streamlines the assessment workflow and presents results through intuitive visualizations.

How We Can Help

Advisory, enablement, and continuous support

Service IT Security supports organizations at every step: from conducting assessments to defining roadmaps and enabling sustainable improvements in SOC operations.
